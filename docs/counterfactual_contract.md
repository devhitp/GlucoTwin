# Counterfactual Engine Contract

## Purpose
This contract defines the interface and safety boundaries for the future Counterfactual "What-If" simulation engine. The Digital Twin will eventually support exploring alternative physiological outcomes based on hypothetical inputs.

## Conceptual Inputs
The simulation engine will accept:
- **Baseline State**: The causal, real-time initialized state vector of the patient at time `T`.
- **Current Observations**: The real-world data observed up to time `T`.
- **Hypothetical Meal Change**: Adjusted timing or quantity of carbohydrate intake at time `t >= T`.
- **Hypothetical Insulin Change**: Adjusted timing or dose of bolus insulin at time `t >= T`.
- **Simulation Horizon**: The forward duration (e.g., +30m, +60m) to simulate.

## Defined Outputs
The engine will return:
- **Projected Trajectories**: The simulated counterfactual glucose path.
- **Risk Trajectories**: The simulated instantaneous risk of hypoglycemia.
- **Delta Analysis**: Quantitative comparison against the projected baseline (no intervention) trajectory.
- **Uncertainty Bounds**: The simulation confidence interval.

## CRITICAL SAFETY BOUNDARY
Counterfactual outputs generated by GlucoTwin are strictly **exploratory simulations for research evaluation**.

Under no circumstances may the outputs be represented, utilized, or interpreted as:
- Medical prescriptions
- Insulin dosage recommendations
- Clinical advice
- Guaranteed physiological outcomes

**GlucoTwin is not, and will not be, an automatic insulin recommendation system or closed-loop controller.**
